# plasma-plots 0.2.3

## Features

New modules in `plasma_plots.theory`, plain numpy and in SI units like `theory.parameters`:

- **`sheaths`**: Bohm speed and current, floating potential (with secondary emission),
  Child–Langmuir space-charge-limited flow (with injection speed), Langmuir–Blodgett flow between
  coaxial cylinders and concentric spheres, Child–Langmuir and matrix sheath widths, OML probe
  currents, ambipolar and Bohm diffusion, Paschen breakdown and mean free paths.
- **`beams`**: perveance, the Child–Langmuir current through an aperture, K-V rms envelopes (closed
  form in a drift, RK4 with space charge and focusing), thermal beam divergence, the Pierce angle,
  the Davisson–Calbick aperture lens, beam attenuation and stripping, space-charge compensation
  time, and `emittance_growth_space_charge`.
- **`magnetron`**: the Hull cutoff of planar and coaxial gaps, Brillouin flow, Hall parameter,
  critical ionisation velocity, E×B speed, the Simon–Hoh and gradient-drift modes, and
  `lower_hybrid_drift`.
- **`collisions`**: the NRL Coulomb logarithm and collision frequencies, Spitzer resistivity, the
  Spitzer and Stix slowing down of a fast ion, its critical energy and stopping, the pitch-angle
  evolution of a slowing-down ion, and the shielded beam-driven current.
- **`negative_ions`**: cross sections and Maxwellian rate coefficients for the destruction of H⁻
  (electron detachment, mutual neutralization, collisions with H and H₂), the Debye length and Bohm
  speed of an electronegative plasma, H⁻ survival through a gas layer and surface-production
  current.
- **`equilibria`**: the Harris current sheet, the Bennett pinch, Solov'ev tokamak equilibria
  (Cerfon–Freidberg shapes), Lundquist force-free fields and the Taylor state. Each is checked
  against its own force balance.
- `kinetic.electron_cyclotron_drift`: the electron cyclotron drift (modified two-stream)
  dispersion relation for electrons drifting across B, as branches.
- `special.bessel_j`: Bessel functions of integer order in plain numpy.

## Documentation

- The theory guide has figures for the sheath, beam, magnetron and collision modules.
- The new **Gallery** shows example scripts and the figures they make, including Child–Langmuir
  against Langmuir–Blodgett, Paschen curves, K-V envelopes, fast-ion slowing down, the Hull cutoff
  and gradient-drift growth.
