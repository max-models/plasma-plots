# plasma-plots 0.2.0

## Features

- **Field lines** (`plasma_plots.fieldlines`, `plasma_plots.fieldline_plots`):
  `B.plasma.analysis.field_lines()` traces field lines through the mapped grid of any
  three-component field (Struphy's `eta` grids, GVEC's and DESC's flux coordinates) and returns
  a Dataset over `(s, line)` with each line's rotational transform, toroidal transits,
  connection length and the punctures of a poloidal section. On it:
  `lines.plasma.plot.poincare()` (Poincaré plots in physical or logical coordinates, colored
  per line, by ι, by connection length or by class; `islands=True` labels the island chains
  with their `n/m` and widths), `.field_lines()` (projections and 3-D), `.footprint()` and
  `.connection_length()` for open field lines; `lines.plasma.analysis.poincare_section()`,
  `.rotational_transform()`, `.classify_field_lines()` (surface, island, chaotic),
  `.islands()`, `.footprint()`, `.seed_grid()`. A field along the lines:
  `phi.plasma.analysis.sample_along(lines)`, `.parallel_wavenumber()` and
  `phi.plasma.plot.along_field_lines(lines, k_parallel=True)`. A flux surface unfolded over
  its angles with straight field lines of slope ι, or traced ones:
  `phi.plasma.plot.surface_map(rho=0.5, iota=ev.iota)`. `B.plasma.plot.poincare()` traces and
  plots in one call.
- **Boozer spectrum and quasi-symmetry**: `mod_B.plasma.analysis.boozer_spectrum()` (the real
  harmonics `B_mn(ρ)` of a quantity on a Boozer grid), `.quasisymmetry_error(helicity="QA")`
  (`"QA"`, `"QP"`, `"QH"` or `(M, N)`: the symmetry-breaking content relative to `B_00`), and
  `mod_B.plasma.plot.boozer_spectrum(helicity=...)` with the error in a second panel.
- **Magnetic topology**: `A.plasma.analysis.critical_points()` finds the O-points and X-points
  of a 2-D flux function (sub-cell accurate, at every time), `.reconnected_flux()` follows the
  flux between an O-point and its X-point over time (the tearing-mode or GEM curve), and
  `A.plasma.plot.critical_points()` marks them on the flux contours.
- **δf and loss diagnostics** for marker Datasets: `orbits.plasma.plot.weight_histogram()`
  (the weights at several times, with their statistics), `.marker_density()` (where the
  markers are against what they represent, and a reference profile), `.lost_fraction()`,
  `.loss_map()` (initial phase space, confined markers grey, lost ones colored by loss time);
  `orbits.plasma.analysis.weight_statistics()` (mean, spread, the relative noise of the total
  and the effective number of markers), `.marker_density()`, `.lost_fraction()`, `.loss_map()`
  (`x="energy", y="pitch"` with `absB`).
- **Rank boundaries**: the new `overlays={"rank_boundaries": {...}}` on `plot_slice` (and the
  other slice-based plots) draws dashed lines at given coordinate values, styled apart from
  `coordinate_lines` so both can share an axis. For data split by [mpiarray](https://github.com/max-models/mpiarray),
  `Layout.boundary_values(axis, coordinate)` gives the values to pass; `rank_boundary_color`
  sets their color (default red).

Every new plot takes `backend="plotly"` and `backend="tikz"`.

## Fixes

- Notebooks keep showing figures inline after a plasma-plots plot. When a kernel's first figure
  was drawn by plasma-plots, leaving its style context turned IPython's interactive mode off
  again, and every later figure, including plain Matplotlib ones, was no longer displayed.
- Plain Cartesian fields (dims such as `("x", "y")`, no logical coordinates) work as the
  `background` of `paths`, `scatter` and `animation` of marker Datasets; they needed `X`, `Y`,
  `Z` coordinates before.
- `markers.plasma.plot.scatter()` draws both axes to the same scale only when the two variables
  have the same units, so a phase space such as `x` against `vx` is no longer squashed; the new
  `equal_aspect=` sets it explicitly.
- Marker paths, quantities and orbit grids name markers by their `marker` coordinate (their id)
  instead of their position in the Dataset.
