## Time series with a growth-rate fit

```python
energy.plasma.plot.timeseries(logy=True, fit=(0.0, 2.0))
```

Overlays an exponential fit on a window of the series and reports the rate in
`result.fit_results`. See [Diagnostics](/plasma-plots/guides/analysis/) for
the damping-rate variant (fit the envelope of an oscillating signal instead).

![A time series with an exponential growth-rate fit overlaid](../../../assets/figures/timeseries_growth.png)

The data behind this plot: `energy.plasma.data.timeseries(other_run_energy)` returns the validated list of series; the fit is `.plasma.analysis.growth_rate()` (see [Selecting data](/plasma-plots/guides/data/)).

## Several series in one axes

```python
energy.plasma.plot.timeseries(other_run_energy, logy=True)
```

Passing further arrays plots them together; if they come from different runs
(different `attrs["run_name"]`), each line is labeled by its run.

## Several plots in one figure

`plasma_plots.figure(nrows, ncols)` makes a figure of panels; every plot method that takes
`ax=` draws into one of them. Here the growing energy with its fit above, and the relative drift
of the total energy below:

```python
import plasma_plots

with plasma_plots.figure(2, 1, sharex=True) as fig:
    energy.plasma.plot.timeseries(fit=(0.0, 2.0), ax=fig[0])
    total.plasma.analysis.drift().plasma.plot.timeseries(logy=False, ax=fig[1])
fig.save("energies.png")
fig.results[0].fit_results[0].rate     # each panel's result, with its fits
```

![The field energy with its fit above, the drift of the total energy below](../../../assets/figures/composed_energies.png)

Panels keep their own colorbars and legends; `sharex`/`sharey` share the axes (and, in Plotly,
their zoom), and `title=` goes above all panels. Panels left empty are hidden.

<details>
<summary>The same plot with Plotly</summary>

`backend="plotly"` finishes the figure as one interactive Plotly figure (see
[Interactive plots with Plotly](/plasma-plots/guides/plotly/)); the plots inside the block draw
into their panels as before:


## Exact and expected curves

`reference` draws exact or expected curves over the series, dashed and black:
a function of `t`, an array on the same times, a `(t, values)` pair, or a dict
of labels to these. A label starting with `_` stays out of the legend. For a
damped Langmuir wave, whose k = 1 amplitude comes from
[`project_mode`](/plasma-plots/guides/analysis/#mode-amplitudes-by-projection),
against the exact envelope:

```python
amplitude = e1.plasma.analysis.project_mode(dim="eta1", number=1)
amplitude.plasma.plot.timeseries(
    logy=False,
    reference={"±0.1 e^(-γt)": lambda t: 0.1 * np.exp(-gamma * t),
               "_lower": lambda t: -0.1 * np.exp(-gamma * t)},
)
```

![The k = 1 amplitude of a damped wave inside its exact envelope](../../../assets/figures/reference_timeseries.png)

## Measured against theory

`against_theory` plots measured values over a parameter against a theory
curve, with their relative error in a second panel. The measured values can be
a 1-D array over the parameter, such as the frequencies from
[`trace_branch`](/plasma-plots/guides/analysis/#tracing-a-branch) over `k` or
growth rates over mode numbers. They can also be an `(x, y)` pair, or a dict of
several runs or methods. The theory can be one function or a dict of several:

```python
traced = spectrum.plasma.analysis.trace_branch(bohm_gross, k_range=(1.5, 5.5))
traced.omega.plasma.plot.against_theory({"Bohm-Gross": bohm_gross, "cold plasma": lambda k: 1 + 0 * k})

from plasma_plots.plotting import plot_measured_vs_theory
plot_measured_vs_theory({"run A": rates_a, "run B": (m, rates_b)}, theory, logx=True, show_error=False)
```

The error panel compares every measured series with the first theory function.

![Traced frequencies against the Bohm-Gross relation, with relative errors](../../../assets/figures/measured_vs_theory.png)

## Comparing two runs directly

```python
field.plasma.plot.compare(reference_field, mode="difference")
field.plasma.plot.compare(reference_field, mode="ratio")
```

Aligns the two arrays and plots their difference or ratio as a 1-D lineout —
useful for a convergence study or comparing a run against a reference.

![Difference of a diagnostic between two runs](../../../assets/figures/compare_difference.png)

![Ratio of a diagnostic between two runs](../../../assets/figures/compare_ratio.png)

The data behind this plot: `field.plasma.data.compare(reference_field, mode="ratio")` returns the aligned difference or ratio as a 1-D array (see [Selecting data](/plasma-plots/guides/data/)).
